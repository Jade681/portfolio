# Interviewer Reflection

Use this after a real interview, whether or not a final hiring outcome is known. Interview the interviewer one focused question at a time; do not send the full questionnaire at once.

## Reconstruct before judging

Start with the most decision-relevant moment:

1. Which answer most increased or reduced your confidence, and what exact evidence caused that change?
2. Which follow-up revealed something the first answer concealed or failed to show?
3. Which question produced little useful evidence, and why?
4. Where might your first impression, similarity preference, nervousness or fluency bias have affected interpretation?
5. Which important dimension was missed, rushed or only inferred?
6. Did the candidate reveal that the role profile or recruitment wording was incomplete or wrong?
7. What would you keep, change or remove before interviewing the next candidate for the same role?

Ask the next question based on the prior answer. Preserve exact recollection separately from later interpretation.

## Produce four outputs

### Case record

What happened in this interview: questions, evidence, decision, uncertainty, candidate experience and follow-up needs. Keep identifiable information in the restricted case folder.

### Interviewer learning

What the interviewer learned about listening, timing, follow-up choice, bias, note-taking and panel coordination. This is about the interviewer's behavior, not a second evaluation of the candidate.

### Role-model update

Whether actual answers exposed a missing task, misunderstood requirement, incorrect weight, hidden working condition or flawed red line. Mark each update as confirmed, plausible or still uncertain.

### Skill iteration candidate

Only reusable method changes. Record observation, previous behavior, narrow change, expected effect and remaining validation. Do not turn one unusual candidate or one awkward question into a universal rule.

## Archive contract

Link the reflection to the role scorecard, interview plan, restricted candidate record, outcome and next-round changes. Keep the reusable learning note anonymized. If the knowledge-base path is available, store the case record in the relevant project/recruitment cycle and the method update with the interviewer Skill notes.
