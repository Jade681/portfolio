# Evaluation and Interview Record

## Evidence ledger

| Time/question | Dimension | Question summary | Answer evidence | Level | Positive signal | Risk/uncertainty | Follow-up status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Preserve short original phrases when material, but avoid unnecessary personal information. Separate observation from interpretation.

## Anchored dimension scale

- **4 — strong evidence:** multiple specific role-relevant examples or a strong work sample; contribution and results withstand follow-up.
- **3 — sufficient evidence:** at least one credible relevant example; minor gaps do not undermine the central claim.
- **2 — mixed/partial evidence:** some relevant behavior, but important ambiguity, shallow depth or inconsistent transfer.
- **1 — weak evidence:** mostly abstract, irrelevant or unsupported claims after fair probing.
- **N/A — unassessed:** insufficient opportunity or information; do not score as 1.

For each dimension provide score, confidence, strongest evidence, main counterevidence and unknowns. A weighted total may summarize but must not replace judgment.

## Consolidated assessment

1. Assessment scope: role, format, materials, duration and omissions.
2. Recommendation: advance / hold for more evidence / do not advance, with confidence and conditions. For candidate practice, use “current readiness” rather than pretending to decide for an employer.
3. Dimension table: rating, confidence, evidence and risk.
4. High-confidence strengths demonstrated under assessment.
5. Risks and unresolved questions, including eligibility/logistics.
6. Written-test review, if applicable: output, reasoning, accuracy, tradeoffs, instructions and AI-use boundary.
7. Truthful answer-strategy improvements without invented achievements.
8. Preparation priorities ordered by decision impact and effort.
9. Compact archive-ready interview record.
10. Candidate-facing feedback and its delivery boundary when the process allows feedback.
11. Offer the interviewer-reflection interview; do not perform it as a generic checklist dump.

## Decision safeguards

- Do not average away a failed mandatory condition.
- Do not let charisma compensate for unrelated missing core evidence.
- Do not double-count the same story across dimensions without noting overlap.
- Distinguish no evidence collected from negative evidence observed.
- State when a recommendation depends on inference or incomplete sources.
- Compare candidates against the role scorecard, not against personalities or backgrounds.
- Base the judgment on evidence after fair clarification and independent re-verification, not on the first answer or first impression.
- Treat a process-specific one-vote veto as a separately defined red line with recorded evidence; never invent a veto rule from an ordinary low score.

## Panel synthesis and archive

Merge notes by evidence, not vote count. Resolve disagreements against the answer or recording; retain minority evidence when unresolved. The lead interviewer owns the synthesis.

Archive: company/team, role, date/stage, format/duration, interviewer roles, JD and candidate sources, scorecard version, questions and concise evidence, recommendation/confidence/open conditions, candidate questions/two-way-fit observations, follow-up actions, decision owner and privacy note. Store recording links and identifiable candidate records only in the restricted case file, never in a reusable method note.
