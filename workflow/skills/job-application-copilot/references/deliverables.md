# Consolidated Application-Preparation Document

For a new recruitment notice, produce one document with the following seven numbered sections. Keep raw facts, analysis and user decisions distinguishable. Use plain language before technical or recruitment terminology.

## 1. 看懂岗位

Start with four concise answers:

- 这个岗位主要解决什么问题？
- 最重要的三项能力是什么？
- 哪些条件必须满足？
- 招聘方在面试中可能考察什么？

Then include the analysis table:

| Conclusion | Type | Priority | Short JD excerpt | Reasoning from excerpt | Fact/inference |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|

Type values: responsibility, must-have, preferred, logistics, business context.

Follow with core business problem, users and scenarios, upstream/downstream relationships, keywords, and unknowns to confirm.

Every major conclusion must trace to a short exact excerpt. Do not use a paraphrase in the excerpt column. Keep quotations limited to the words needed for verification. If the conclusion requires product or recruitment judgment beyond the text, explain that step and label it as inference.

## 2. 匹配个人经历

| Role requirement | Match strength | Personal evidence | User action | Result/artifact | Boundary or missing fact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Match strength values: strong, partial, weak, missing.

For every experience, preserve source, the user's actual action, result or artifact, and AI/team contribution boundary.

## 3. 判断优势与缺口

Use three explicit subsections:

1. 有真实经历证明的能力
2. 自己认为具备、但证据还不充分的能力
3. 目前明显的缺口

Explain the evidence or absence of evidence behind every judgment.

For every major risk, add:

- 投递前怎么补强
- 面试前准备什么证据
- 暂时补不齐时如何诚实表达

## 4. 判断是否值得申请

Give a reasoned recommendation, not a final decision. Include:

- 我相对岗位的最大优势
- 我相对岗位的最大能力或条件风险
- 这个岗位对我的最大价值
- 这个岗位对我的最大风险或代价
- 需要向招聘方确认的问题
- 投递简历前最需要准备的事项
- 综合建议与适用条件

Present the assessment as two directions:

1. 我为什么可能适合岗位，以及招聘方为什么可能不选我。
2. 岗位为什么可能适合我，以及我为什么可能不该选择它。

Do not use a match number as the conclusion and do not decide on the user's behalf.

## 5. FAB 自我介绍

Use the role analysis and evidence table to draft a selective first-impression introduction. Adapt the same method when the user is not a candidate but needs to introduce themselves in a role-related setting.

Start with a short strategy note:

- 场景与听众
- 想留下的第一印象
- 选择突出的 2—3 个真实依据
- 不应该夸大或暂时不提的内容

Then provide:

| Version | Time | Draft | FAB mapping | Evidence used | Difference from other versions | Notes or risks |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

Default versions: 1 minute, 2 minutes and 3 minutes. Keep each version speakable, natural and proportional to the occasion. Do not exceed three minutes unless explicitly requested.

After the table, add a compact comparison that explains:

- 1 分钟版保留了什么、删掉了什么，适合什么场合；
- 2 分钟版相对 1 分钟版新增了什么；
- 3 分钟版相对 2 分钟版新增了什么，以及什么时候不建议使用。

FAB emphasis should state:

- Feature: the identity, experience, skill or responsibility being presented;
- Advantage: why it matters in this exact context;
- Benefit: what value it gives the listener, team, recruiter, interviewer or candidate.

FAB mapping must point to the actual content in the draft. For each version, briefly identify which sentence or information point is F, which is A, and which is B, so the user can see how the introduction was constructed.

Use only verified evidence. If the best introduction would benefit from missing facts, add a short "可补充信息" line instead of inventing details.

## 6. 简历调整建议

Before producing a final targeted resume, explain:

- content to retain and emphasize;
- experiences to move earlier or later;
- wording to rewrite and why;
- content to shorten or omit;
- facts that require confirmation;
- unsupported claims that must not appear.

When the full workflow includes resume generation, follow the recommendations with a complete role-specific resume content draft. Do not make the user infer the resulting resume from advice alone. Keep unresolved facts omitted or visibly marked for confirmation.

Before drafting the header, resolve any decision-changing logistics in the JD. Ask about supported work location, earliest start, duration and weekly attendance. Do not copy the current city when it may be read as inability to work onsite. Use a confirmed availability statement when relevant, or omit the location until confirmed.

After the draft, present the evidence gaps in priority order and let the user choose whether to improve them. A lack of time changes the level of detail, not the obligation to produce the final resume from verified facts.

When a final resume is requested, produce:

Produce:

1. A separate targeted resume, normally one page unless the user requests otherwise.
2. A claim audit that maps each new or materially changed bullet to its evidence source.
3. A change log with selected, omitted, reordered and reframed content.
4. A short list of unresolved facts requiring user confirmation.

Do not overwrite the master resume.

After the user approves the factual content, run the final copy through humanizer in embedded mode, then typeset and verify the resume through kami. Record both passes in the change log. Humanizer must preserve all claims; kami must report output formats, page count, build checks, font result, visual result and remaining `[DATA NEEDED]` gaps.

For every included project, check the evidence index for a public URL. Add a clickable descriptive hyperlink when one exists and verify its target before delivery. Do not fabricate or expose private links. A real application run must deliver finished application files unless the user explicitly requests a draft or simulation stop.

## 7. 十个高概率面试问题

Provide exactly ten questions, ordered by likelihood and importance:

| Question | What it assesses | Verified experience to use | Likely follow-up | Current preparation gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|

If no verified experience fits, say so and state the preparation action. Do not invent a story.

### Live practice protocol

The table is the preparation artifact, not the live interaction format.

- Coached drill: ask one question, receive one answer, give specific feedback and a revised answer, then continue.
- Preserve each original answer and its revised version.
- Realistic simulation: call the user-provided interviewer Skill; do not reveal or correct answers during the interview.
- Written test: give the full test in one batch and evaluate after the batch response.
- Interview: one question and one answer at a time, with consolidated feedback only after the simulation ends.

### Additional preparation checklist

Group items by company/product and role understanding; three strongest evidence stories; gaps and honest responses; product and technical fundamentals; logistical confirmation; and questions for the interviewer.

Mark each item ready, needs clarification, needs practice or needs learning.

Include an interview-delivery checklist:

| Item | Prepared content | Evidence or source | Risk if unprepared | Practice action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Cover these items when relevant:

- STAR plus difficulty: Situation, Task, Action, Result, plus the real obstacle, constraint or tradeoff that made the story credible.
- Company understanding: company/product/users/stage, why this role matters, and one specific reason for interest.
- Speaking pace: slower opening, shorter sentences, pauses after key points and a clear one-sentence answer before details.
- Interviewer connection: natural shared ground, active listening and specific follow-up without forced flattery.
- Unexpected follow-ups: changed assumptions, backup paths, small validation tests and tradeoff explanation.
- "How could this be more perfect?": next improvement, needed evidence, current constraint and priority.
- "Why this role?" and "what if passion fades?": durable motivation beyond temporary enthusiasm, including contribution, learning value, responsibility and user/team value.
- Feedback responsiveness: what feedback was received, what exact artifact or answer was revised, and what improved.

## Real test record

Record the date and target role; raw input and source; personal sources used; key outputs from each workflow stage; user decisions and corrections; screenshots or artifact paths; failures or weak outputs discovered; and revisions made to the workflow.

## Iteration note

Use this compact structure:

- Observation from practice
- Previous workflow behavior
- Change made
- Reason for change
- Expected effect
- What still needs validation
