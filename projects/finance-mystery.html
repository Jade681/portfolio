金融题材六人互动剧本杀Six-player Finance-themed Mystery Game

北京大学暑期课程《Python 语言基础与人工智能应用》的四人小组项目，围绕金融题材制作的六人互动剧本杀，配一支约两分钟的宣传视频。 A four-person project for Peking University’s summer course “Python and AI Applications”: a six-player finance-themed interactive mystery game, with a roughly two-minute promo video.

打开项目页 →Open the project →

为什么做Why I made it

课程要求以小组形式完成一个可交付的互动项目；我们选择用金融题材做一款六人互动剧本杀，把课上学到的方法用在一个完整的作品上。 The course asked each group to ship a complete interactive project. We chose a six-player finance-themed mystery so we could apply what we had learned to one finished piece of work.

我做了什么What I did

我担任组长，负责组员协调与进度；独立制作约两分钟的网站宣传视频；协调项目介绍表与项目报告；组织试玩测试；并借助 AI 生成自动化测试脚本。 I was the group lead — coordinating the team and the schedule. I independently made the two-minute promo video, coordinated the project write-up and report, organised playtesting, and used AI to generate an automated test script.

AI 帮了什么How AI helped

AI 辅助宣传视频的制作，并根据描述生成自动化测试脚本。 AI assisted with the promo video and generated the automated test script from a description.

我负责的判断What I decided

我判断宣传该突出什么、试玩中发现的问题是否要改、脚本测试覆盖到哪里，并对小组的交付负责。 I decided what the promo should emphasise, which playtest issues to act on, how far the script tests should reach, and I was accountable for the group’s delivery.

网站主体与主要 AI 搭建由另一名组员完成；这里只记录我负责的组织、宣传与测试部分。宣传视频为本地文件，暂未单独公开。 The site itself and its main AI build were done by a teammate; this page covers only the organisation, promo and testing I led. The promo video is a local file, not yet published separately.
