「寻味漫记」播客Flavours in Memory — Podcast 中 / 英两期CN & EN episodes

一个以地方美食文化为主题的播客系列，包含中文和英文两期不同采访主题的节目（不是互译版本）。英文一期涉及家乡食物、biryani、义乌「鸡毛换糖」等地方小吃与贸易故事。 A podcast series on regional food culture, with separate Chinese and English episodes on different interview topics (not translations of each other). The English episode covers hometown food, biryani, and local trade stories such as Yiwu’s “feathers for sugar.”

为什么做Why I made it

我想用声音记录地方美食背后的人和故事，也借这个系列练习中英双语的策划与主持。 I wanted to use audio to record the people and stories behind regional food, and to practise planning and hosting in both Chinese and English.

我做了什么What I did

我参与选题策划、嘉宾联络、多人主持，以及音频剪辑与配乐；中英两期分别围绕不同的采访对象和主题。 I took part in topic planning, guest outreach, multi-host recording, and audio editing and scoring; the Chinese and English episodes each centre on a different guest and theme.

AI 帮了什么How AI helped

AI 辅助部分音频处理与整理。 AI assisted with some of the audio processing and organisation.

我负责的判断What I decided

我判断哪些片段保留、节奏是否顺，以及双语表达是否自然。 I decided which segments to keep, whether the pacing worked, and whether the bilingual delivery sounded natural.

两期均为成品候选版本，此前未在平台正式发布，因此不列收听数据。 Both episodes are candidate final cuts that were not formally released on a platform, so no listening figures are given.
