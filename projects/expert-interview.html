行业专家访谈Industry Expert Interview 中英字幕bilingual subtitles

诺丁汉卓越奖课程项目 “Building Talent in Ningbo’s Globalized Industries” 的行业专家访谈视频，围绕职业转型与专业探索。 An industry-expert interview video for the Nottingham Advanced Award project “Building Talent in Ningbo’s Globalized Industries,” on career transitions and choosing a field.

为什么做Why I made it

课程项目需要一支面向行业专家的访谈视频，帮助同学理解职业转型和专业选择。 The course project needed an interview video with an industry expert, to help classmates understand career transitions and how to choose a field.

我做了什么What I did

访谈前，我收集组员的问题，用 AI 工具分类、去重并排序，形成访谈提纲；访谈后，我独立为整段视频制作中英文字幕。 Before the interview I gathered the group’s questions and used an AI tool to cluster, de-duplicate and order them into an interview outline. Afterwards I produced the full Chinese–English subtitles on my own.

AI 帮了什么How AI helped

AI 辅助把零散问题整理成结构化提纲。 AI helped turn a scattered list of questions into a structured outline.

我负责的判断What I decided

我判断哪些问题重复、哪种顺序更合理，以及字幕的断句和译法是否准确。 I decided which questions overlapped, which order made more sense, and whether the subtitle segmentation and translation were accurate.

视频为本地文件，是否公开展示待确认。旧简历中「提升效率 / 专业度」一类说法缺少量化证据，这里不作此表述。 The video is a local file; whether to show it publicly is still to be confirmed. Earlier résumé phrasing about “efficiency / professionalism gains” lacks measurable evidence and is not repeated here.
