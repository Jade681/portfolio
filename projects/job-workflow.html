求职面试 AI Skill / 工作流AI Skill and Workflow for Job Applications and Interviews

把岗位分析、个人证据、简历匹配和面试官视角检查整理成一套可重复使用的求职流程。 A repeatable workflow connecting role analysis, personal evidence, résumé matching, and interviewer-perspective review.

为什么做Why I made it

求职信息分散、判断步骤多，也很容易把 AI 的推测误当成事实。我希望建立一套既能提高效率、又能保证表达真实的流程。 Job-search information is fragmented and requires many judgement calls. It is also easy to mistake AI inference for fact. I wanted a process that improved efficiency while keeping every claim accurate.

我做了什么What I did

我整理个人经历与证据，拆解岗位要求和匹配关系，建立简历事实核验流程，加入面试官视角检查，并把反复使用的方法沉淀为 Skill 和工作流。 I organised personal experiences and evidence, broke down role requirements and their matches, created a fact-checking process for résumé content, added an interviewer-perspective review, and turned repeated methods into a reusable Skill and workflow.

AI 帮了什么How AI helped

AI 辅助提取信息、整理结构、对照岗位和执行重复检查。 AI supported information extraction, structuring, role comparison, and repetitive checks.

我负责的判断What I decided

我确认事实是否真实、哪些经历可以使用、哪些说法属于过度推断，并决定最终的简历表达和求职策略。 I verified facts, decided which experiences were relevant, identified overstatements, and made the final decisions on résumé wording and job-search strategy.

展示的是方法和流程；个人履历库与完整面试记录不公开。 This shows the method and the workflow; the private evidence library and full interview records are not published.
