03 / AI Practice

北辰青年四周 AI 实践Four-Week AI Practice with Polaris Youth

从认识自己、理解团队和用户，到制作招募物料，再到建立求职与面试 AI 工作流的一次完整实践。 A complete practice cycle: understanding myself, the team, and users; creating recruitment materials; and building AI workflows for job applications and interviews.

为什么做Why I did it

我希望不只是零散地向聊天机器人提问，而是学习怎样把 AI 放进真实任务，形成可以重复使用、持续迭代的工作方式。 I wanted to move beyond isolated chatbot questions and learn how to integrate AI into real tasks through repeatable, continuously improving workflows.

我做了什么What I did

我阅读 34 名成员已开放的名片墙与个人说明书，整理团队特点；参与小组网站"核心特点"模块的内容与交互构思；检索并整理 9 条小红书定性样本；指挥 AI 制作 8 张校园行动招募海报并逐页核验；制作复盘模板，并逐步搭建求职 Skill、面试官视角检查流程和个人知识库。 I reviewed the shared profiles and personal guides of 34 team members to identify team characteristics; contributed to the content and interaction concept for a team-website module; collected and organised nine qualitative Xiaohongshu samples; directed AI in creating eight campus recruitment posters and checked each one; created a reflection template; and gradually built a job-search Skill, an interviewer-perspective review process, and a personal knowledge base.

AI 帮了什么How AI helped

AI 辅助资料整理、文案与视觉草案、页面代码和工作流执行。 AI supported research organisation, copy and visual drafts, page code, and workflow execution.

我负责的判断What I decided

我负责定义问题、选择呈现方式、检查事实和职责、判断视觉层级，并根据团队反馈调整结果。 I defined the problems, chose presentation approaches, checked facts and responsibilities, judged visual hierarchy, and revised the work based on team feedback.
